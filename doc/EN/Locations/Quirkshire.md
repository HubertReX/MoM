# Info

The **city** under which the **library** is hidden, where the all-knowing [[Miss Information]] holds office. [[Barman Absinthrayner]] is the one who tells you about it. It lies in the [[Kingdom of Knaveshire]], some ==2== days' journey north of [[Blunderhaven]]. Past the [[Tangled Forest of Annoyance]] you have to turn **east**.
