# How to get there?

The city is somewhere to the north. "_Somewhere_" is decidedly not enough to set off on.

**Success**: Two days north, past the [[Tangled Forest of Annoyance|Tangled Forest of Irritation]], then turn east. The [[Barman Absinthrayner|barman]] has never been to the city, but he knows the route by heart.
