# The fish the cat ate

The [char]cat[/] has eaten the [[Fresh fish|fish]] supply. To the [[Barman Absinthrayner|barman]] this is not a catering problem, it is an incoming **civil war**!

The [char]cat[/] keeps the [[Fresh fish|fish]] in a [item]chest[/] hidden somewhere behind the bushes. You just need a weapon strong enough to cut those bushes down. Unless there is some other way to get hold of the [[Fresh fish|fish]].

After that all it takes is carrying the [[Fresh fish|fish]] back to the [[Barman Absinthrayner|barman]].

**Success**: There is fish, there are snacks, the patrons are content - another **crisis** averted.
