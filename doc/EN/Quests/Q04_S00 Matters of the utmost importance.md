# Matters of the utmost importance

There are a few matters the [[Barman Absinthrayner|barman]] would like you to sort out for him. These are not _run-of-the-mill_ matters - these are matters of the **utmost importance**!

**Success**: What you pulled off leaves the [[Barman Absinthrayner|barman]] eternally grateful. It translates into lower prices at [[the Lost Cork Tavern|the tavern]] and beer served in cleaner tankards. Which is not to say the tankards are entirely clean...

## Notes

The umbrella's title is ironic, because this whole village is one big joke: to the [[Barman Absinthrayner|barman]], village business ranks with international diplomacy.
